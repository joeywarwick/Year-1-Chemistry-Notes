## 2. Intro to mechanisms
- Most reactions occur between a electrophile and a nucleophile.
	- Nucleophiles donate electrons.
		- They are similar to bases.
	- Electrophiles accept electrons.
		- They are similar to acids.
- Electrons are transferred from the nucleophile to the electrophile, which results in the formation of a new bond.
> Mechanisms can be predicted with rules that do not require rote learning.
