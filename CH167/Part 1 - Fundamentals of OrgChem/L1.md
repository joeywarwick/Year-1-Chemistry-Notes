## 2. Drawing molecules
- Organic molecules are typically represented using skeletal formulae.
- It is done like A-Level, but benzene is better represented with Kekule structure of alternating C-C and C=C bonds.
- An addition is representing 3D structure.
	- A wedged bond shows the bond pointing out of the page
	- A hashed bond shows the bond pointing in the page
	- A normal bond shows the bond on the plane of the paper
