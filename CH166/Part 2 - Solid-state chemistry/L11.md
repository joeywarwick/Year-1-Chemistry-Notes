## 2. Ionic solids' structures
### 2.1. Solids with ionic ratio is 1:1
- Ionic solids have a crystalline arrangement allowing for the identification of a unit cell.
- There are a few common structures of crystalline ionic solids:
	- Rock salt structure:
		- The ions each have a FCC arrangement. For an example, in KCl the K<sup>+</sup> and Cl<sup>-</sup> ions are each in their own **FCC arrangement.**
			- Because of this we can say that the chlorides **occupy all of the octahedral holes**.
			> We know chloride occupies all of the holes due to the KCl formula, because one octahedral hole per potassium ion, and one chloride per potassium ion.
		- Examples:
			- NaCl
			- KCl
		- To identify this, you will find an ion bonded to **six other counterions** as the bond distance.
			- The bond distance is **half of the cell parameter.**
		- They take this structure generally due to ions of the same charge **repelling**.
			-  We want to prevent the same ions from going in contact, so the counterion must be **larger than the hole**
			- Because of this, we want the ions to be of as **similar size** as possible. 
	- CsCl structure:
		- We need to consider **packing efficiency** in addition to ion size.
			- Cs<sup>+</sup> and Cl<sup>-</sup> have very similar sizes.
		- Despite the structure appearing similar to BCC, the ions are different, resulting in **simple cubic Cs<sup>+</sup> with Cl<sup>-</sup> in the middle**.
		- This is more efficient than the FCC.
			- This is because the Cl<sup>-</sup> is surrounded by 8 Cs<sup>+</sup>.
		- This also leads to greater interaction between ions of the opposite side.
		- The hole size for simple cubic is ~72.3% of atom. So this is efficient when the ion size rations are sufficiently large.
> **NOTE** The two above structures can coexist - in KCl, as the size ratio is around the 72.3% threshold.
	- Zinc blende (Sphalerite) structure:
> There are twice as many tetrahedral holes than atoms.
- The ratio between zinc and sulphide ions is 1:2, 
	- So we know that zinc occupies **half** of the **tetrahedral** holes.
- This structure is viable where the size of ion is > than 22.5%. 
### 2.2. Solids with ionic ratio greater than 1:1
- CaF<sub>2</sub>, **fluorite** structure:
	- Calcium is FCC arranged.
	- F fills **all** of the **tetrahedral** holes.
> This structure is preferred for 1:2, even if then ratio is larger than 41.4%. This is because it is bonded to eight adjacent ions in fluorite structure, whereas just 4 in CsCl.
- Li<sub>2</sub>O, **antifluoride** structure
	- Fluorite structure but with the cation is greater quantity.
