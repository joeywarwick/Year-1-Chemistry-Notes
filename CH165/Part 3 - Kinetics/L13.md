## 2. Arrhenius equation
- The rates of most elementary equations increase as the temperature increases.
	- k depends on temperature.
- The Arrhenius equation models the temperature factor on the value of k.
	- $k = Ae^{\frac{E_a}{RT}}$
		- A is the pre-exponential factor, and has the same units as k.
### 2.1. Graphical representations
- The Arrhenius tells us that the rate coefficient (k) increases exponentially with temperature.
- If we take the natural logarithms of both sides of the equation:
	- $\ln{k} = ln{A} - \frac{E_a}{RT}$
- We can see that a plot of ln k 1/T gives a straight line with gradient -E<sub>a</sub>/R and intercept ln A.
